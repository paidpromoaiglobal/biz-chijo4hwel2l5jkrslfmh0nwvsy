Our Story

Made with Passion

Hotlix Food and Drinks is Deorali's go-to destination for a satisfying meal and a relaxed evening out. Nestled in the iconic Ropeway Building along Tibetlogy Road, we offer a welcoming atmosphere where locals and travellers alike come together to enjoy hearty food and chilled beverages. Whether you are grabbing a quick lunch or settling in for a long evening with friends, Hotlix promises quality, comfort, and great taste every single time.

With a strong rating of 4.3 out of 5 and a loyal community of guests, we take pride in serving dishes and drinks that keep people coming back. Open every day from 10:30 AM to 11:00 PM, we are perfectly positioned for your midday meals, evening snacks, and late-night cravings. Come experience the flavours and warmth that have made Hotlix a beloved landmark in Gangtok's vibrant food scene.
